# My map

A film and photography portfolio on a map of Canada. Move the cursor to travel, hover a marker to zoom in, and click to open a project.

The whole site is one file, `index.html`. To add your own work, edit the `OWNER` and `PROJECTS` lists near the top of its main script: give each project a `pos: [latitude, longitude]` and point its media at your own images or videos with `src: "media/your-file.jpg"`.

Map data: Natural Earth (public domain).
